## 2. Proposed synthesized Analysis brief

### Project and performance gap

Representatives need to make and explain consistent decisions for two supported complaint categories using the current guide. In the fictional audit, eight of twenty initial decisions did not match the guide. The intended improvement concerns correct performance; the intervention has not been chosen. [SOP-01; EVID-01]

### Evidence and possible causes

The audit establishes errors in the example sample, not their causes. Four of six observed representatives could not locate the guide unaided. When provided with it, four applied both rules correctly and two still confused the routes. Resource access and rule application warrant investigation; no conclusion about motivation, capacity, or the need for training follows automatically. [EVID-01; EVID-02]

### Audience and work environment

Newly assigned representatives have ticketing-system familiarity and variable routing experience. Work is desktop-based with digital references available in principle. Design must accommodate the documented language/access needs and verify actual reference access. [CTX-01 §1; EVID-02]

### Stakeholders and constraints

Carry the support lead, SME, and quality lead roles, review availability, turnaround assumptions, relative milestones, and production boundaries into Design. Confirm the production budget and delivery/collection arrangements. Do not choose an intervention or authoring tool from license availability alone. [CTX-01 §§2–3]

### Evaluation plan

Retain L4-1, L3-1, and L2-1 with their defined evidence methods, proposed criteria, responsible roles, and explicit baseline/timing/access gaps. The audit's 60% initial-routing accuracy is a sample L3 baseline only; it is neither an L2 baseline nor an observed L4 result. L2-1 is a defined candidate pending route applicability and designer confirmation, not an unspecified learning measure.

### Open questions and assumptions

1. Do the observations sufficiently distinguish guide-access issues from knowledge/application needs? What additional evidence would change the route decision?
2. Is the guide correct and usable for the defined exercise boundaries? Validate the new source details for this fictional walkthrough before treating them as the exercise baseline.
3. What operational targets, reporting access, evaluation owners, and opportunity-based timing can be confirmed? Unknown L3/L4 details remain visible.
4. What delivery/collection approach and production resources are feasible? These remain Design decisions.
