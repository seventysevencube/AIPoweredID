Blocker = must fix before real pilot users; Major = material risk or significant requirement gap with a workaround or narrow trigger; Minor = local defect, inconsistency or document error; Note = bounded risk or observation. - **Hard limits respected:** no paid AI calls, no production database or service access, no sign-in emails, no writes to the live site. The live site could not be reached at all from this environment (see §6).

## 1. Plain-language summary

- **Verdict: not yet ready to invite real pilot designers, but close. No blockers were found.**
- The core protections held when tested: one designer cannot open or change another designer's project, guest links show only what was shared and stop when turned off, the $20 AI limit is enforced by the database, and AI drafts never overwrite your work.
- **Top three problems:** (1) open demo sign-up is documented as still switched on, which lets anyone create an account and use the shared AI budget; (2) the "what you may add" notice about permitted material appears only on the Sources page, not where designers first paste material in Analysis; (3) PowerPoint exports put long lists on one slide, so the text runs off the slide.
- Two more things to do before inviting people: review real AI output in the live check you already planned, and do a hands-on accessibility check, including the exported slides.
- **Compared with October 17:** that audit reviewed plans. None of its 5 blockers still blocks the pilot: 2 are fixed, and 3 are partly fixed. Of all 31 earlier findings, 6 are fixed (4 fixed outright, 2 settled by your decisions), 12 are partly fixed, 7 no longer apply, 6 remain deferred by your decision, and none got worse.
